2. What AdAgent is

AdAgent is a technical automation tool. Given a listing-search or listing-page URL that you submit, and that falls within a supported domain, AdAgent:

  1. Retrieves the publicly visible content of that page (and, for search-result URLs, the individual ad pages linked from it);
  2. Sends the extracted text to a third-party large language model (LLM) provider for automated analysis; and
  3. Presents you with a generated report, including an AI-assigned score (1–10), narrative analysis, flagged risk items (“red flags”), and — for multi-ad searches — a comparative summary/leaderboard.

Supported sources. AdAgent currently accepts and processes URLs only from olx.pl, otomoto.pl, and otodom.pl. Submissions from any other domain are rejected. We may add or remove supported sources at any time without notice.
