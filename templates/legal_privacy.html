2. Scope

This Policy applies to:

  • Visitors to adagent.dimosense.com;
  • Registered users (account holders authenticated via magic link);
  • Pro subscribers; and
  • Any individual whose information appears in a listing you submit to the Service for analysis (a “third-party data subject” — e.g., a private seller on OLX, Otomoto, or Otodom).

3. What personal data we collect

3.1 Data you provide directly

Data When collected Why

We do not use passwords. Authentication is entirely via one-time “magic link” email tokens (valid for a short, limited time) and a resulting session cookie. We never ask for or store a password.

3.2 Data collected automatically

  • Session cookie (adagent_session): an HttpOnly, Secure, SameSite=Lax cookie containing a random session token, used solely to keep you signed in. It is not used for advertising, analytics, or cross-site tracking, and we do not use third-party analytics or advertising cookies/pixels on the Service.
  • Standard server/web logs generated by our hosting and reverse-proxy infrastructure (e.g., IP address, timestamp, requested URL, user agent) for security, abuse prevention, and troubleshooting.

3.3 Payment data

If you subscribe to AdAgent Pro, your payment card details are collected and processed entirely by Stripe, Inc. through Stripe Checkout. AdAgent never receives, transmits, or stores your card number, CVV, or other card data. We store only the Stripe-generated identifiers necessary to administer your subscription (Stripe customer ID, subscription ID, price/plan ID, and subscription status), plus the email address associated with the subscription.

3.4 Third-party listing data (data about people other than you)

AdAgent’s core function is to retrieve publicly accessible listing pages from URLs you submit on olx.pl, otomoto.pl, and otodom.pl only — the Service technically refuses to process URLs from any other domain. When you submit a listing search or ad URL, we automatically extract:

  • The ad title, price, description text, spec/parameter table, and a general location field (typically a city or district, as published on the listing — not the seller’s home address);
  • In some cases, the broader visible text of the ad page, used as a fallback so our extraction can pick up fields that vary by site layout or language.

This content may incidentally include a seller’s name, phone number, or other contact details if the seller chose to type them into the public ad text. AdAgent does not click “reveal phone number” buttons, does not scrape seller profile pages, and does not attempt to enrich, cross-reference, or build a profile of any seller. Data is extracted solely to generate the report you requested and is not repurposed for any other individual’s benefit.

Legal basis for this processing under GDPR: this data was made publicly available by the seller on the source marketplace (Art. 6(1)(f) — legitimate interest in operating an ad-comparison tool on data the subject chose to publish, balanced against minimal intrusiveness: no scraping of hidden/gated data, no resale, no profiling). Where AdAgent acts at your direction to analyze specific ads you selected, we also act as your processor for that data, with you as the (informal) controller of your own search decisions.

3.5 Data sent to our AI provider

The listing content described in 3.4 (title, price, description, location, parameters, and relevant page text) is transmitted to OpenAI (via the OpenAI API) to generate the AI score, red-flag analysis, and summary you requested. See Section 6 for details on this sub-processor.

5. Data retention

  • Reports (your submitted URL, scraped listing content, and AI analysis output) are stored in our production database and, for certain report types, as generated HTML files on our server, so that you (and, for anonymous/unauthenticated searches, anyone holding the unguessable report link) can revisit them. We currently retain reports indefinitely unless you request deletion — there is presently no automatic time-based purge of report content. If you want a report (including any third-party listing data it contains) deleted, contact us at the address in Section 1 and we will delete it manually.
  • Account data (email, Stripe identifiers) is retained for as long as your account exists, plus the period required by Polish tax/accounting law after account closure (generally 5 years for invoicing-related records).
  • Session tokens expire automatically after 7 days of issuance (or on logout) and are periodically purged.
  • Magic-link tokens are single-use and expire after 15 minutes; used/expired tokens are retained briefly for audit purposes.
  • We are working toward shorter, automated retention windows for report data and will update this Policy when that is implemented. If you are a data subject (including a third-party seller) who wants listing content about you removed sooner, see Section 9.

8. Cookies

AdAgent uses a single essential cookie:

  • adagent_session — HttpOnly, Secure, SameSite=Lax. Strictly necessary to keep you signed in. Cannot be disabled without breaking sign-in.

We do not use analytics cookies, advertising cookies, or third-party tracking pixels. Because this cookie is strictly necessary for the Service to function, it is exempt from consent requirements under the EU ePrivacy rules, and no cookie banner is required for it alone. If we add non-essential cookies (e.g., analytics) in the future, we will update this Policy and implement appropriate consent.
